document-ocr-engine

Finds and reads every word on a photo of a receipt or a document, then flattens the page. I wrote and trained the detector and the reader from scratch. Both run in your browser, so your photo never leaves this tab.

How it works

  1. Find the words

    A DBNet detector marks which pixels belong to text. Each blob of text pixels becomes a rotated box around one word.

  2. Read each word

    Every box is cut out, straightened to 32 pixels high and read by a CRNN: a small CNN, then a two-layer BiLSTM, decoded with CTC.

  3. Put the words in order

    Words are chained into lines by their direction, height and spacing. Lines that share a row are joined, then sorted top to bottom.

  4. Flatten the page

    The paper is found from the colors around the detected words, and a homography maps its four corners onto a flat rectangle.

Both models were trained from scratch on a CPU, on synthetic text plus the FUNSD and CORD training sets. The detector has 1.3M parameters and the reader 2.5M. Here they run as ONNX files on ONNX Runtime Web and give the same words as the Python version.
